Our story

Rooted in Dagoretti. Built by coaches and families who believe in the game.

Who we are

An academy, a community, a pathway.

Santo Soka Academy is a Kenyan football academy based in the Dagoretti area of Nairobi. Founded in 2010 — the year stitched into our crest — we are a standalone academy built by coaches and families from the neighbourhood.

We train boys and girls from age 4 up to 20, with a full pathway from Under-6 all the way through to our Senior team. The academy focuses on nurturing talent, discipline and teamwork, giving every player a real chance to develop their skills and pursue their aspirations in the game.

Under the guidance of our technical staff, the academy has seen success in local tournaments and continues to play a pivotal role in our community.

Our Coaches

Licensed, local, and in it for the players.

The academy is led by Head Coach Humphry, who sets the direction for every age group — from the first touch at Under-6 all the way to senior football. Our coaching staff live and work in the Dagoretti community and hold FKF / CAF / FIFA-recognised coaching qualifications.

We invest in our coaches because we know sessions are only as good as the person running them. Members of the technical team recently completed the FKF Introductory Level Coaching Course, with more certifications in progress.

Alumni

From Dagoretti to the South Sudan Premier League.

The academy pathway is already producing professional footballers. Khan Jal Kume, a winger developed through Santos Soka, now plays top-flight football in the South Sudan Premier League for El Merreikh SC Bentiu — proof that the work being done on the dusty pitches of Dagoretti can carry a player into the professional game.

Stories like Khan's are what every Under-6 turning up for their first session is ultimately training towards.
