Under-6

Little Stars

Ages 4–6Boys & girls2 sessions / week

Where the journey starts. Our Under-6 programme introduces football through fun, games and movement. No pressure, no scoreboards — just a love for the ball.

  • Coordination, balance and agility
  • First touch and ball familiarity
  • Play-based coaching
U6
Under-8

Foundation

Ages 7–82 sessions / weekSmall-sided games

Ball mastery is the focus. Players learn dribbling, turning and basic passing through small-sided games that keep the ball at their feet as much as possible.

  • Dribbling and 1v1 work
  • Introduction to passing and receiving
  • Friendly matches vs other academies
U8
Under-10

Development

Ages 9–10Weekend fixturesPositional intro

Players begin to understand positions and team shape. We build on foundations with passing patterns, combinations and the first taste of competitive weekend football.

  • Positional awareness
  • Passing and combination play
  • Weekend friendlies and youth tournaments
U10
Under-12

Futures

Ages 11–12Local leaguesTechnical focus

Technical detail sharpens, with dedicated sessions on receiving under pressure, scanning and first-touch direction. Our U12 side plays in age-appropriate local leagues.

  • Technique under pressure
  • Scanning and decision making
  • Local youth league fixtures
U12
Under-13

Cubs

Age 13Competitive fixturesLime home kit

Our Under-13 side — instantly recognisable in the lime-green home kit — steps up into age-group competitive football. This is the year players start being asked to repeat standards under pressure: physical, technical and in the way they carry themselves off the pitch.

  • Game-model training sessions
  • Competitive youth fixtures in Nairobi
  • First proper scouting from the U14 and U16 coaches
U13
Under-14

Elite Youth

Ages 13–14County levelS&C introduced

Positional specialisation begins. Players follow age-appropriate strength and conditioning protocols and compete at county-level tournaments.

  • Role-specific training
  • Strength, mobility and conditioning basics
  • County tournaments
U14
Under-16

Academy

Ages 15–16RegionalMatch analysis

Players are introduced to tactical periodisation, match analysis and individual development plans. Scouts and partner clubs begin to watch the group closely.

  • Tactical periodisation
  • Video analysis of key moments
  • Exposure to regional competitions
U16
Under-18

Reserves Pathway

Ages 17–18Trial pathwaySenior integration

The bridge between the academy and senior football. Our U18 squad trains alongside the senior team and provides the core pool for promotion into the Santo Soka Senior team.

  • Integrated sessions with the senior squad
  • Professional training loads
  • Pathway into the Senior team
U18
Senior Team

Santo Soka Senior Team

18+Men's footballNairobi

Where academy graduates step into men's football. The Senior side is the top of our pathway, fielding a core of players who came up through Santo Soka's age groups in Dagoretti.

The team plays a mix of competitive and friendly fixtures in and around Nairobi.

  • Destination for academy graduates
  • Community-centred matchdays
  • Full-team training sessions
