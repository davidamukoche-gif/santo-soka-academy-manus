About the academy

Grassroots football, done the right way.

Santos Soka Academy is a Kenyan football academy based in the Dagoretti area of Nairobi, founded in 2010. We are a standalone academy rooted in our community, with a full pathway from the first touch at Under-6 to senior football.

Our academy trains boys and girls from age 4 to 20, giving every player a real pathway — from their first ever session in an Under-6 shirt all the way to the Senior team.
